The loudest the miner may get. Press Listen to hear it from where you sit (the minimum is fixed at 15 %).
During the whole setup the heater's own fan rule drives the fans live, between 15 % and this maximum (up 5 % when chips reach 74 °C, down 2 % when they stay ≤ 70 °C; at most one move a minute during setup), so chips are measured at the temperature they will run at. Each level's fan = where the rule is when that level finishes (its starting fan later).

Drive points

Board voltage per trim drive: line = the 0-100 % trim sweep on the conservative test profile (~250 MHz, Braiins' minimum voltage; one point per 2 %), dots = the floor (red: the sweep's 100 % point), the trim test of older runs (grey) every search step (one colour per level) and the load droop points (square: your lowest wattage, diamond: your highest). Each level's first step starts from these points; steps are drives (no voltage feedback) and each one adds its averaged voltage here.
